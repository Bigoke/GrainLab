# GrainLab privacy policy

_Last updated: 10 November 2026_

GrainLab is a photo editor and camera. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## What the app uses

| Permission | Why | Where the data goes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Camera | Taking photos and videos with film looks | Saved only to your phone's Pictures/GrainLab and Movies/GrainLab folders |
| Microphone | Recording sound with videos | Saved only inside the video file on your phone |
| Location (optional, off by default) | Adding where a photo was taken to the photo's details | Written only into the photo file; never sent anywhere |
| Photos you pick | Editing photos you choose with the Android photo picker | Processed on your phone; edits are stored in the app's private storage |

## What the app does not do

- It does not upload your photos, videos, edits or templates to any server.
- It has no account, no advertising and no analytics.
- It does not share data with third parties.

## Crash reports

If the app closes unexpectedly, a crash report (technical details, no photos) is saved on your
phone. You are asked whether you want to share it; nothing is sent unless you choose to.

## Templates you share

When you share a template, the app creates a small file or QR code containing only the edit
settings. You decide where to send it.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at https://github.com/Bigoke/GrainLab.
